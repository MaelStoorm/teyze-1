Animal sound effects - provenance and licenses
=============================================
All five files are real field/studio recordings (not synthesized). All are CC0 1.0
(public domain dedication) at the original recording. No attribution is legally
required for any of them. Optional credits are listed below anyway.

Processing applied to every file (by us): downmix to mono, resample to 44100 Hz,
DC removal, trim leading/trailing silence, peak-normalize to -3 dBFS,
20 ms linear fade in/out, 16-bit PCM WAV.

Note: freesound.org could not be reached from the build container, so the
original freesound pages were NOT checked directly. The licenses come from the
per-file credit tables in the GitHub repos named below.
