### Full description

```
Welcome, dear! Auntie Fatma is waiting for you.

Fatma Teyze ("Auntie Fatma") is a cosy Turkish neighbourhood game you can open for two minutes on the bus, on the metro or during a tea break. Fatma Teyze and her neighbours give you small errands every day: buy tomatoes at the bazaar, carry a message next door, find the lost cat. Stroll around the neighbourhood, lend a hand and earn cookies.

No rush, no timers, no penalties for mistakes. Big text and big buttons make it easy for children and grandparents alike.

WHAT'S IN THE NEIGHBOURHOOD

• A living neighbourhood: the aunties' houses, Uncle Ahmet's coffeehouse, the bazaar, a farm, a pond, a stream, a flower garden, beehives, an apple orchard and a sunflower field.
• Fatma Teyze's errands: bazaar shopping, passing on news, the lost cat, cooking and the "gold day" tea party.
• Bazaar shopping: visit the greengrocer, the bakery and the deli and tick off your list.
• Neighbours: Filiz, Miyase, Hülya Teyze and Uncle Ahmet. Help them out, earn friendship hearts and receive gifts.
• Sütlü: the yellow-and-white neighbourhood cat. Feed her and she will follow you.
• Vegetable garden (bostan): plant today, harvest tomorrow, share with your neighbours.
• Day and weather: morning, evening and night follow your phone's clock; some days it rains.
• Backgammon (tavla): play a round with Uncle Ahmet at the coffeehouse.
• Tea brewing: help Filiz Teyze brew the perfect Turkish tea.
• My Home (Evim): spend your cookies on rugs, a sedir sofa, a samovar, a cuckoo clock and decorate your home.
• Gift calendar: a small gift on your first visit each day and a surprise pouch on the seventh day.
• Create your character: girl or boy; hair, skin, clothes and colours.
• Shop: handy gifts and decorations for the neighbourhood.

COMFORTABLE FOR EVERYONE

• Walk by tapping the screen or with the joystick at the bottom left.
• Make the text bigger in the settings.
• No internet needed; play on a plane, on the metro, anywhere.
• No ads, no account, no personal data collected. Your progress is saved only on your phone.

The tea is brewed and the cookies are in the oven. Come on, the neighbourhood is waiting!

The game is in Turkish.

MaelStoorm Studios
```

## Veri güvenliği (Data safety) formu – cevaplar

Play Console > **Uygulama içeriği > Veri güvenliği**.

1. **Uygulamanız gerekli kullanıcı veri türlerinden herhangi birini topluyor veya paylaşıyor mu?**
   → **Hayır.**
   (Oyun internete bağlanmaz, analiz/reklam/çökme raporu SDK'sı yoktur, hesap yoktur. İlerleme yalnızca cihazda, uygulamanın kendi klasöründeki `kayit.cfg` dosyasında saklanır ve cihazdan hiç çıkmaz. Google'ın tanımına göre cihazdan dışarı gönderilmeyen veri "toplanan veri" sayılmaz.)
2. "Hayır" seçilince şifreleme ve silme talebi soruları sorulmaz; form doğrudan özete geçer.
3. Özette mağazada görünecek ifade: **"Veri toplanmıyor"** ve **"Üçüncü taraflarla veri paylaşılmıyor"**.

Kontrol listesi (forma "Hayır" demeden önce):
- [x] `export_presets.cfg` içinde INTERNET izni kapalı (özel izin listesi boş).
- [x] Oyunda reklam, analiz, Firebase veya benzeri eklenti yok.
- [x] Kullanıcı yedeği (`user_data_backup/allow=false`) kapalı; kayıt Google Drive'a da yedeklenmez.
- [ ] İleride uygulama içi satın alma, reklam veya analiz eklenirse bu form ve gizlilik sayfası **güncellenmeli**.
